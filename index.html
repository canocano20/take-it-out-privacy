Privacy Policy

Last updated November 19, 2026

This policy explains how Vaulter Games handles information when you use the Android game Take It Out! – Arrow Puzzle.

Game progress and settings

You can play without creating an account or signing in. The game saves progress, results, hint balances and preferences such as language, audio and vibration in app storage on your device. Vaulter Games does not operate a cloud account for this data. You can remove the local save by clearing the app’s storage in Android settings. Clearing storage removes your progress and preferences from the device.

Advertising

We use Unity Ads to show ads between levels and offer optional rewarded ads. Unity Ads may collect and share the following information with advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention partners.

  • IP address, approximate location inferred from it, and device or advertising identifiers.
  • SDK identifiers and technical diagnostics used to run and measure the ads service.
  • Ad views, taps and other interactions with ads. Unity Ads may also process app usage or purchase signals when supported by its advertising and measurement features.

Unity uses this information to deliver and measure ads, provide app and ad functionality, analyze performance, and prevent fraud and abuse. Unity says that the data its Ads SDK collects is encrypted in transit. Unity and its partners process the information according to their own privacy terms. Unity’s Game Player and App User Privacy Policy

Your privacy choices and deletion requests

Use the Data Privacy icon in a Unity ad, where available, to review advertising choices and request access to or deletion of data collected by Unity Ads. You can also contact Unity’s privacy team. Android settings let you manage the device’s advertising ID. These choices can change ad personalization while the app continues to show ads.

Unity retains advertising data under its privacy policy for as long as needed for its stated purposes and legal obligations. Local game data stays in app storage until it is cleared or removed with the app. If you contact Vaulter Games, we use the email address and details you provide to respond to your request and keep the correspondence for as long as needed to handle it or meet applicable legal obligations.
